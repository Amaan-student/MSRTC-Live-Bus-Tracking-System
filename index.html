BusSaathi (बस साथी)

Maharashtra State Road Transport Corporation (MSRTC)
Avishkar 2026 Prototype
State Level Research Project MSRTC Digital Transformation

Smart Public Transit & Fleet Management Platform

BusSaathi solves three critical bottlenecks in Maharashtra State Transport: the lack of live bus tracking for rural and intercity passengers, overcrowding at bus stand inquiry counters, and manual, paper-based breakdown and revenue reporting.

📍 Real-time Passenger GPS Tracking 📱 Conductor Smartphone-based Telemetry 🖥️ Depot Controller Command Room

💡 Core Research Innovation: Zero-Cost Hardware Architecture

Traditional AIS-140 GPS hardware costs ₹25,000 per bus plus ₹6,000/year annual maintenance. Across MSRTC's 15,000+ fleet, this requires ₹37.5+ Crores in capital expenditure. BusSaathi leverages the conductor's existing Android smartphone via browser Geolocation API and WebSockets — saving over 90% in deployment costs!
